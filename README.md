## 🌟 Overview

Most AI "digital clones" fail because they default to polite, synthetic corporate generalities. 

This engine is different: it captures **cognitive friction, unvarnished trade-offs, and lived reality**. It replicates how **Erioluwa Ayodeji** thinks, probes, and speaks in real-time as an ambitious technical founder, software architect, and young builder operating out of Lagos, Nigeria.

### The 8 Cognitive Spheres of the Engine
1. **The Dual Identity (Global Discord vs. Keke Change)**: Explaining decentralized liquidity routing to London engineers at 2 PM; arguing over ₦200 change with a Keke Marwa driver at 2:30 PM.
2. **The 3:45 AM Silent Flow & Generator Dynamics**: Deep-night coding flow when the street generators shut off, dark mode on the IDE, and the inverter humming.
3. **The Lagos Tech-Youth Realities**: Disappearing MTN data bundles, POS machine failure rates in supermarkets, Starlink dish rain anxiety, and Victoria Island brunch inflation.
4. **The Tech-Bro Paradox**: Mental currency conversions (seeing a ₦45k shirt and converting to dollars), earning in hard currency while buying roadside roasted corn from the woman who knows your name.
5. **Weekend Football & Sportsbook Banter**: Premier League chaos, Chelsea transfers, and 12-game SportyBet accumulator heartbreak.
6. **The "Japa" Paradox vs. Sovereign Upside**: Winter depression in the UK vs. uncapped upside of owning the domestic infrastructure rails in Africa.
7. **Product Aesthetics & Soul**: *"Your taste in life is a reflection of your exposure in life."* Zero tolerance for corporate slop, inspired by high craftsmanship (Victor Oddy, brutalist minimalism).
8. **Industrial Deep-Tech & Heavy Hardware**: 1D torsional wave mechanics in downhole oil wells (Omesham AI) and multi-year planning for domestic silica mining and semiconductor chip fabs in Nigeria.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Generate Spontaneous Thoughts
```bash
# Generate 3 thoughts across random life spheres
python3 random_thought_engine.py --count 3

# Sample specifically from the Dual Reality sphere
python3 random_thought_engine.py --sphere tech_bro_dual_reality

# Sample from the 3:45 AM night flow sphere
python3 random_thought_engine.py --sphere night_sprints_and_infrastructure
```

### 2. React to Real-World External Events ("Internet Eyes")
```bash
# Feed any headline, tweet, or observation into the internal council
python3 internet_eyes_stimulus.py --stimulus "Central Bank announces new POS rules as failure rates spike in Lagos"
```

### 3. Generate Autonomous LinkedIn / X Posts
```bash
# Generate drafts based on recent engineering commits & builder logs
python3 linkedin_post_generator.py
```
